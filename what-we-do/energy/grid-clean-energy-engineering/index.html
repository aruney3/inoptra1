Engineering Capabilities

  • Transmission Engineering: Overhead line routing, structural tower design and corridor studies
  • Substation Engineering: AIS/GIS layouts, protection schemes and primary/secondary design
  • Distribution Engineering: Grid modernization, feeder automation and underground cabling
  • BESS Engineering: Battery storage integration, thermal management and PCS layouts
